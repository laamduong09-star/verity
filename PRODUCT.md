# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Young Vietnamese-American adults at the start of their financial life — first paycheck, first credit card, first time thinking seriously about saving and investing. Bilingual (EN/VI), often navigating financial decisions their family may not have explained to them in English. The job to be done: understand basic financial mechanics (compound interest, credit, recommendations, splitting money with family) well enough to act on them, without needing a finance background.

## Product Purpose

A financial literacy toolset for first-time earners: a compound interest calculator today, with planned modules for recommendations, jargon explanations, credit education, and family money-splitting. Success looks like a user walking away understanding *why* a number changed, not just seeing the number — confidence to make a decision, not just data.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, clear, encouraging. Calm confidence rather than hype — explains rather than impresses. Bilingual by design (EN/VI), so warmth and clarity have to read in both languages, not just English wordplay.

## Anti-references

Not a stiff corporate banking/brokerage dashboard (cold navy/gray, dense unexplained tables, jargon with no definitions). Not a gamified fintech app (confetti, hype-driven number pop animations, casino-like urgency) — money decisions should feel calm and considered, not thrilling.

## Design Principles

- Explain, don't impress — every number or chart earns a plain-language "why" nearby.
- Calm confidence over hype — no urgency tactics, no gamification of financial decisions.
- Bilingual parity — EN and VI content get equal visual weight and equal care, never a translated afterthought.
- Plain language first — define financial terms inline rather than assuming prior literacy.
- Show the mechanism — favor visualizing *how* money grows (charts, breakdowns) over just stating outcomes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast and motion compliance (respect `prefers-reduced-motion`). Copy written for readers with low financial literacy — avoid jargon, or define it inline/on-hover. Bilingual EN/VI throughout, not just on the surface.
